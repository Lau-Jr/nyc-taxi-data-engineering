# Datasheet: NYC yellow-taxi zone-hour demand

Following *Datasheets for Datasets* (Gebru et al., 2021). This covers the ML feature table
`features_zone_hour` and the warehouse it is built from. Maintainer: Lau-Jr. Last updated
2026-10-03.

## Motivation

- **Purpose.** To forecast hourly taxi pickups per NYC pickup zone for the next day, so a
  fleet operator can position vehicles. Built as coursework for DSAI 6226 (MSc Data
  Science & AI, NM-AIST), Unit 8.
- **Created by** the pipeline in this repository from public data. No funding, no client.

## Composition

- **Instances.** One row per **TLC pickup zone × hour**, from 2026-01-01 00:00 to
  2026-02-28 23:00. That is 262 zones × 1,416 hours = **370,992 rows**. An hour with no
  pickups is a row with `target_trips = 0` (36% of rows), not a missing row.
- **Columns.** Keys, 10 features, the target `target_trips` and a `split` label. Each one
  is described and leakage-audited in
  [docs/phase8/leakage_audit.md](docs/phase8/leakage_audit.md).
- **People.** No row describes a person. Rows are counts of trips per zone and hour; the
  smallest unit is "N pickups in zone Z between 08:00 and 09:00".
- **Split.** By time: warmup, train, validate, test. See
  [docs/phase8/split_strategy.md](docs/phase8/split_strategy.md).

## Collection and processing

- **Origin.** NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission (TLC) yellow-taxi trip records, monthly
  parquet files for January and February 2026, published as open data. The records are
  reported by the taxi technology vendors (Creative Mobile Technologies, Curb, Myle, Helix).
- **Pipeline.** Raw parquet → Unit 6 quality checks (hard failures quarantined: 45,077 in
  January, 40,613 in February) → `fact_trip` (7,039,065 trips) → `sql/08` → feature table
  → dated snapshot in `data/features/<version>/` with `manifest.json` (sha256, config,
  source pipeline runs, git commit). Full hop-by-hop lineage is in the README.
- **Rebuild.** `python -m src.features` (or `make features`) runs from the warehouse in
  about 10 seconds. It is deterministic: the same `fact_trip` and config give a
  byte-identical parquet.

## Consent, privacy and the PDPA

- **Consent.** Passengers did not consent to this specific use. The data is released by
  TLC for public reuse, and no new data is collected from anyone.
- **Personal data.** Trip-level records are treated as potentially personal: pickup and
  dropoff time and place together can single out a person (see the README's PDPA
  assessment, Unit 6). **This table removes that risk by aggregation.** It keeps no
  individual trips, no dropoff locations and no amounts, only counts per zone-hour. Even a
  zone-hour with a count of 1 reveals only that *someone* was picked up there, which is
  what TLC itself publishes.
- **PDPA (Tanzania, 2022).** The data subjects are in the United States, so the Act
  applies only in a limited way. Its principles are still applied: a stated purpose (above),
  minimisation (aggregated counts only) and retention (deleted at the end of the
  semester). The feature snapshots are git-ignored as data; only their manifests are
  versioned.

## Known gaps and limitations

- **Only two months.** There is no seasonal change, and train covers just 24 days.
- **Data delay.** The lag features assume yesterday's trips are available at midnight (a
  live operator feed). With TLC's public files, which arrive about 2 months late, they
  would not be. See the leakage audit.
- **Excluded trips.** Trips that failed hard quality checks (about 1.2%, mostly
  `dropoff <= pickup`) are not counted, so `target_trips` slightly under-counts real
  pickups.
- **Optional-field gaps.** About 29–30% of trips lack passenger count and other optional
  fields. They are still counted as trips, because this table only counts trips.
- **Unusual days.** 25–26 January show a sharp drop in trips, likely weather. No weather
  data is included. Holidays: 1 Jan (warm-up), 19 Jan (train), 16 Feb (validate).
- **Zones are ids.** No zone names or geography are included: no TLC zone lookup ships
  with this data. Zones 264/265 are TLC's "Unknown" and "Outside NYC" buckets.
- **Yellow taxis only.** No green taxis or ride-hail (Uber/Lyft) trips, so this is not
  total NYC demand.

## Intended uses

- **Intended:** zone-level, next-day demand forecasting and baseline comparison, and
  teaching about feature engineering, leakage and time-based splitting.
- **Not intended:** pricing or fare prediction, anything about individual drivers or
  passengers, forecasting total NYC mobility, or forecasting zones or periods outside the
  grid.

## Maintenance

Regenerated by `make features` whenever new months are ingested. Each regeneration
creates a new dated snapshot; existing snapshots are never edited. Changes to features
or split boundaries are made in `config/features.toml` / `sql/08` and noted in the
leakage audit.
